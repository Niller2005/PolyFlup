# Strategy analysis - paper run 2026-10-27 (~4 h, 15m + 5m, BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP)

Data: `trades_paper.db` (paper_fills, trades, shadow_signals) and `logs/trades_paper.log`.
Reproduce with `.opencode/skill/strategy-analysis/` (`slug_pnl.py`, `pair_outcomes.py`, `log_events.py`).
Caveat: paper maker fills use the conservative `cross` model (a bid fills only when the market trades *through* it), so adverse selection is probably overstated; live behaviour is unmeasured.

## Where the account went
Start 1000 -> cash 462.7 + open positions at cost 248.1 = 710.9, i.e. about **-289 at cost**. The `trades` table shows only -56.8 because leg-outs never become rows. Taker fees on emergency sells: 36.

| Source | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Completed pairs held to the end | **+15** on 14 trades, 0 losers | trades table |
| Pairs where a leg was sold pre-settlement | **-67 vs +32 had they been held** (51 trades, 4 losers, ~-99 swing) | trades table: locked edge vs realised pnl |
| Leg-outs (one leg filled, other timed out, sold) | about **-5.3 per leg-out, -13.7c/share**, ~100 of them | log + fills |
| Leg-out rate | **48.6%** of 216 pairs (break-even at 1c edge is ~7%) | `pair_outcomes.py` |

## Findings
1. **Pre-settlement exit of a hedged pair has non-positive EV by construction.** The losing leg is priced at q at T-60..-180s; q already is the market's flip probability. Selling it at the bid, paying `0.07*q*(1-q)` fee, converts a locked +1c pair into a directional bet. Example: BTC-5M 1793141700, Down sold at 0.13 at T-63s, Down then won, Up redeemed at 0 -> -31. Hold-to-resolution pairs: 14 trades, 14 winners.
2. **Maker pairs lose money at a 1c edge.** EV per attempt = 0.51*(+1c) - 0.49*(13.7c) = about **-6c/share**. Leg-outs are the adverse-selection signature: the filled leg is the one the market is moving away from (60 of 101 filled legs were the lower-priced one). Tuning cannot fix this: wider edge (combined 0.95-0.97) just fills less (68-100% leg-out).
3. **Hedge timeout: no change.** 5m markets already time out at 30-40s (scaled by window length); only 15m markets wait 90s. For 15m, 11 of 39 completions took >35s and 27 timed out, a ~29% completion hazard after 35s, which roughly breaks even against the extra ~4c/sh drift of waiting. (An earlier draft of this analysis mixed the markets and wrongly recommended 35s.)
4. **XRP-5M is an outlier**: 73% leg-out (36/49) vs 35-47% for the others.
5. **Late-window mode has no tradeable edge.** Leaders win 72/90/95/100/100/100% by |z| bucket (model slightly underconfident), but 94% of |z|>=1.5 rows have no ask below 0.995, and where an ask exists it is 0.97-0.99 (EV <= 1.5c). The 24 ENTER decisions won **1 of 24**: ENTER fires only when the ask is far below the model, i.e. when our strike/z is wrong and the market right (ask <0.3: 6% hit; 0.3-0.6: 38%). One hypothesis worth more data: asks of 0.88-0.97 won 97% (n=79, 54 windows, +3.6c EV), but that is about 1 sigma.
6. **Hold-after-timeout** rule used a stale entry confidence; held legs lost >90% (fixed in `fix(strategy): only hold ...`; held only 7% of timeouts anyway). The "confidence too low -> sell all" branch is effectively "always sell" (106 of 217 timeouts, 95 more via "neither side clearly winning").

## Recommended changes (ordered by evidence strength)
1. **Disable pre-settlement exit for hedged pairs** (keep only for unhedged positions). No new risk, removes a -99 swing.
2. ~~Cut the hedge timeout~~ - withdrawn, see finding 3.
3. **Stop quoting XRP-5M** (or require a wider market-quality gate) until leg-out is understood.
4. **Retire late-window ENTER unless the market agrees**: require `ask >= p_use - 0.15`; keep logging. Do not enable `LATE_WINDOW_LIVE`.
5. **Measure real maker adverse selection before scaling**: run live at the 5-share minimum for a day and log fill-to-move (price of the filled leg 10s after the fill). If real leg-out loss is under ~3c/share, maker pairs may work; if not, stop the pair strategy rather than tune it.
6. Alternative to explore with data first: flatten immediately at first fill when the other leg's bid is above the trail cap (currently waits up to the timeout).

## Applied (v0.7.2)
- `PRE_SETTLEMENT_EXIT_HEDGED=NO` (default): hedged pairs are held to resolution; unhedged positions keep the exit.
- `LATE_WINDOW_MAX_DISAGREEMENT=0.15`: late-window ENTER is skipped when ask < p - 0.15.
- XRP-5M removed from the user's `MARKETS_PAPER`.

## Follow-ups (2026-10-28)
- **Trailing**: replaying recorded leg-outs showed the second leg's bid sits 3-4c above the old cap; `TRAIL_MAX_LOCK_LOSS=0.03` converts ~40% of them (v0.7.4).
- **Hold vs sell a single leg**: filled leg wins 20-26% vs ~27c sale price - sell and hold are within ~3c/share, and neither entry confidence nor a z-model probability at timeout identifies the winners (n=9 where the model said hold by 10c+: 11% won). A 25% random hold-to-resolution arm (`LEGOUT_HOLD_SHARE`) now measures it (v0.7.5).
