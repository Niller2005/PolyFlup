# Strategy analysis - paper run 2026-10-28 (03:15-08:30 UTC, 10 markets)

Data: `pair_attempts` (660 rows: 337 completed, 139 leg-outs, 177 skipped), `evaluations` (1,838), `market_windows` (420 windows,
~85 distinct window starts), `market_ticks` (37k), trade tape (112k). Reproduce with `.opencode/skill/strategy-analysis/`.

## Verdict
No profitable configuration of the strategy was found. Every segment of the pair strategy loses about 6c/share, the confidence
signal has no measurable edge over the market price, and the unhedged directional idea that looked good in-sample failed
out-of-sample. The bot is therefore best run as a **research instrument at minimum size** until a new hypothesis passes
its pre-registered test.

## Findings
1. **Pair strategy: -6.0c/share/attempt** (479 attempts with a result), negative in every segment I could cut: 5m (-6.7c) and 15m
   (-4.3c), every price band, window quintile, spread, depth, planned edge, size, confidence and UTC hour (`seg.py` in the
   session; all 95% intervals exclude 0 or straddle it from below). Completed pairs now net **-70** (average lock -0.3c in
   the 3c-tolerance hour, -2.4c afterwards) and leg-outs **-303**.
2. **Leg-out rate fell from ~47% to 29%** after the trailing change (v0.7.4) - it works - but the locks it pays cost about as much
   as the leg-outs it avoids.
3. **The replay-optimised tolerance was wrong.** `adaptive.lock_loss` selected the maximum (8c) because its objective never
   charged the worse locks on pairs that complete. Live result: 109 of 338 completed pairs locked at -4c...-8c (average lock
   -2.4c vs -0.3c), and the leg-out rate did **not** improve (25% at 3c tolerance, 26-33% at 8c). Replaced by randomised
   arms compared on realised results (v0.8.2).
4. **Hold vs sell a leg-out: no difference.** Paired on the same 101 sold leg-outs: hold -19.3c, sell -21.8c per share (+2.6c,
   SE 4.5c). The random hold arm alone (n=24): -5.2c/share vs sell -22.4c, which is selection noise at that size.
5. **Market quality differs, none is positive.** Leg-out rate: BTC/ETH/SOL(+5m) 18-23%, DOGE-5M 42%, DOGE 41%, XRP-5M 48%
   (35 of 73). Measured pair EV (adaptive): -6.1c (ETH-5M) ... -10.1c (DOGE-5M).
6. **Signal vs market**: net of ask and fee, over 1,735 evaluations / 62 window starts the edge is -1.1c, 95% CI [-6.7, +4.9].
   Confidence >= 0.6 (159 rows, 43 starts): +6.0c, CI [-2.6, +15.3]; not established.
7. **Directional rule D1 (frozen after the first 50 minutes)**: in-sample +21.4c/share on 40 entries / 9 starts. **Out-of-sample
   (after 04:10 UTC): 153 entries, 49 window starts, hit 44%, net EV -6.5c/share, CI [-18.6, +5.9], -$98.9 at 10 shares.**
   The first hour was a regime; the rule has no edge. (30-minute blocks alternate between +18c and -28c.)
8. **Market structure**: buy-at-ask EV by price bucket (2,490 samples, 64 clusters) is flat to negative; the only +EV cells
   are multiple-comparison noise and a +1.8c cell at ask >= 0.95 (n=69, 23 clusters).
9. **Equity series bug** (fixed): `position_marks`/`account_snapshots` listed an entry leg twice (once from `trades`, once from
   `paper_positions`), so `held` and `equity` before v0.8.2 are inflated (peak 1,559). Cash, fills and `pair_attempts` were not
   affected; realised paper equity is about 670 (-330), almost all lost in the first three hours at full size (leg-outs -214 in
   hours 03-04 alone) before explore sizing took over (5 shares; ~-10 to -30 per hour afterwards).

## What changed in v0.8.2
- Tolerance arms `{1c, 3c, 6c}` drawn per attempt, recorded in `pair_attempts.lock_arm`, best arm chosen after 20 results each
  (30% exploration). Compare with: `SELECT lock_arm, COUNT(*), AVG(CASE outcome WHEN 'both' THEN 1-final_entry_price-final_hedge_price ELSE realized_pnl/size END) FROM pair_attempts WHERE lock_arm IS NOT NULL AND outcome IN ('both','timeout') GROUP BY 1`.
- Snapshot double count fixed.

## Recommendations
1. Keep the pair strategy at minimum size (already automatic) and do not add the directional position-adding feature.
2. Judge the arms after ~100 results each (about 8-12 hours).
3. Next hypotheses worth a pre-registered test on the recorded data: (a) quote only the leg the tape shows is being bought
   (use `market_trades`), (b) the late-window favourite at ask >= 0.95, (c) a real maker-fill test at 5 shares to replace the
   pessimistic paper fill model - the single biggest unknown.
